After the Pro discount expires

Once the 60-minute discount countdown hits zero, what should the dashboard do? Four directions, each with a "Re-arm" control so you can flip the chip back to active and watch the transition.

Approach A

Inline state swap

Chip stays in the header but turns gray. Tap "Try again" to re-arm. Lowest visual cost — easy to miss.

Inline · Quietest

Approach B

Toast slide-in

Chip dims; a top-right toast announces the miss with a "Generate again" CTA. Noticeable but not blocking.

Approach C

Modal lockout

Page dims, modal asks to extend or decline. Highest urgency; saves the conversion but disrupts flow.

Approach D

Auto cooldown

Chip flips to a "Charging…" cooldown bar, then auto-rearms. Gentlest, never blocks the user.

Suggested rollout

  1. Ship Approach B (Toast) as the default — recovers conversions without breaking flow.
  2. Cap the re-arm count at 2 per session so the urgency stays believable.
  3. Track click-through on "Generate again" — if it spikes above ~30%, the discount is too easy to recover and we should switch to Approach C for new accounts.
